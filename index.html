Agro-Processing Export Partnership — West Africa to Trinidad
TF
TradeFarm Co.
Lagos, Nigeria
About this Opportunity

TradeFarm Co. is a Lagos-based agro-processing SME specialising in value-added agricultural exports. We are seeking established Caribbean importers and distributors to carry our range of premium cassava flour, cold-pressed shea butter, and dried spice blends.

Our products hold NAFDAC certification and comply with CARICOM food safety standards. We have successfully exported to the United Kingdom and Germany and are now prioritising South-South trade pathways as part of our AfCFTA strategic growth plan.

What We Are Looking For
  • Established food importers or distributors in Trinidad, Barbados, or Jamaica
  • Minimum order quantity of 500kg per product line — negotiable for first orders
  • Partners with existing retail or wholesale distribution networks
  • Willingness to co-develop localised packaging and branding
What We Offer
  • Competitive FOB pricing with flexible 30/60-day net payment terms
  • Full documentation: certifications, lab results, and origin certificates
  • Dedicated account manager for the first 12 months
  • Exclusive distribution rights available for single-country partners
